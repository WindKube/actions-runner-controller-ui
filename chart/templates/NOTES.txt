Every data source is optional. If a panel says "unavailable", the footer names
which source failed and why — that is the diagnosis, not an error to file:

  * ARC CRDs missing        the controller is not installed, or is a version
                            that does not serve actions.github.com/v1alpha1
  * metrics.k8s.io 403      RBAC. The resources are named `pods` and `nodes` in
                            that API group, NOT podmetrics/nodemetrics; the wrong
                            spelling matches nothing and 403s exactly like a
                            missing metrics-server.
  * listener unavailable    either ARC_UI_LISTENER_METRICS_URL is unset (ARC
                            ships listener metrics disabled) or
                            ARC_UI_CONTROLLER_NAMESPACE points at the wrong
                            namespace — the listener pods are NOT in the runner
                            namespace.
